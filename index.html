⚙️ Parent settings

Hidden from your daughter: hold the ⚙️ button for about 1.5 seconds to open this panel.

📦 Add a story pack

A story pack is a .zip (or a folder) with a story.json and its pictures, MP3s and subtitles. Pick one or more; a story with the same code is replaced. Use ⬇️ Pack on a story below to make one.

Story packs you add are kept on this tablet and work offline.

💬 Subtitles

English lines come from an en.json file in the story's folder on the website. Stories without one just show Dutch.

Subtitles come from the story text. With an MP3 they follow its timing file (story.vtt) if there is one, otherwise they're estimated. Make one in ✏️ Edit → ⏱️ Time the subtitles.

File names (e.g. for the Frozen story)

🎵 frozen.mp3 story · frozen_q1.mp3 question 1 · frozen_q1_answer.mp3 full answer sentence for question 1 · frozen_title.mp3 card title
🖼️ frozen.jpg card picture · frozen_1.jpg, frozen_2.jpg … pictures shown one after another while the story plays · frozen_q1.jpg picture with question 1 · frozen_q1_a.jpg RIGHT answer as a picture · frozen_q1_b.jpg, frozen_q1_c.jpg the two WRONG answers as pictures
story1 instead of frozen also works. Your own stories have a code like eigenxxxx: it's shown when you save them.

➕ New story

Card emoji (shown when there's no card picture) Title (spoken when she taps 🔊 on the card) Story text (read aloud by the voice when there's no MP3; mention the answers in it!)
🎵 Story MP3 (the long audio)
💬 Subtitles (each sentence of the story text is one line)

Tap the green button each time the voice starts the sentence shown in bold. Takes as long as the story.

🖼️ Card picture (on the home screen; also shown while listening if there are no story pictures)
🖼️ Story pictures: shown one after another while she listens, spread evenly over the story. Put them in story order (◀ ▶ to move).
🔤 Woordjes (vocabulary): one word per line, as emoji | word | example sentence. The sentence is optional.

Pictures or MP3s for a word (w1.jpg, w1.mp3 …) come from a story pack or the stories folder. Keep the order the same if the word has one.
