⚙️ Parent settings

Hidden from your daughter: hold the ⚙️ button for about 1.5 seconds to open this panel.

🎵🖼️ Load MP3s and pictures in one go (optional)

Select many audio files and pictures at once. The file name says where each one goes. They're stored inside the app on this tablet and keep working offline. (You can also add them one by one with ✏️ on a story, below.)
